### EVIDENCE RULES
- Record source, publication date, outcome, audience type, attribution method, limitations
- Distinguish purchases, leads, listeners, clicks, and views
- Never label an ad "highest converting" based on views, likes, awards, or runtime
- Do not attribute campaign-wide results to one creative without evidence
- Label proposed ideas as hypotheses
- Extract structure and strategy; create original scripts and visuals

---

### CREATIVE ANALYSIS (for each reference)
Document:
- First-frame visual and opening words
- Audience and emotional trigger
- When the product/artist/brand becomes identifiable
- Product demonstration or proof
- Offer and objections addressed
- Editing rhythm and sound
- Call to action and destination
- What is supported by evidence vs interpretation

### MUSIC CONTENT FOR JUKE THE MOVEMENT
| Format | Execution | Conversion Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Strongest-line performance | Open on strongest lyric, face visible, accurate captions. No long intro. | Qualified listening clicks; saves |
| Relatable POV | "When you finally stop explaining yourself..." + lyric completes thought | Shares, profile visits, listening |
| Story behind the bar | "I wrote this after..." brief truth, then perform section | Deeper fan engagement |
| Creator interpretation | Give creators one excerpt + emotional theme; let them make POV video | Sound use and discovery |
| Live proof | Open on strong real performance moment, identify song + where to hear | Listening or show interest |
| Fan invitation | Show what someone gets in community: early music, BTS, live session | Membership signups |

**Rules:** Choose excerpts from actual songs. Never invent lyrics or personal history. One primary action per ad: listen, save, follow, or join. When music plays under an OTF commercial, it promotes recognition but doesn't auto-convert to music streams. Make a separate music-focused cut with its own listening CTA.

### CLOTHING CONTENT FOR ONLY THE FINEST
| Format | Execution | Conversion Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Fit and detail demonstration | "Here's how Forest Legacy actually fits." Front, side, back, pockets, movement | Purchases; fewer fit objections |
| One set, three looks | Style as full set, jacket separate, pants separate | Product-page visits, purchases |
| Product-first comedy | Clear glimpse of fit first, then briefcase story | Attention + product interest |
| Founder explanation | Explain one specific design decision while showing feature close-up | Trust and purchases |
| Real customer try-on | Customer gives actual size, fit experience, opinion | Purchase confidence |
| Objection-answer video | Answer one real question: sizing, fabric, shipping, daylight look | Convert interested shoppers |

**The Wrong Briefcase:** Make two cuts — cinematic 30s version + shorter product-first version showing Forest Legacy earlier. Waiting for reveal is a creative choice, not a proven sales advantage.

**Rules:** Use exact approved product images and colorways. Do not fabricate reviews, specs, stock scarcity, discounts, or shipping promises. AI imagery must not misrepresent actual garment fit or construction.

### PRODUCTION CONTROLS
- Approve character references, product accuracy, and starting images before animation
- Generate one shot and one variation at a time
- Log model, settings, credit cost, and approved output
- If submission is slow, check job history before resubmitting
- Prepare dialogue, captions, brand assets, and music as separate editing elements
- **Do not launch ads, spend money, contact creators, or publish without authorization**

---

### MEASUREMENT
- Assign unique creative IDs and tracked destination links
- Music: distinguish landing-page visits and outbound DSP clicks from verified listening and saves
- Clothing: track purchases, CAC, revenue, returns, and contribution after product + fulfillment + payment + ad costs
- Services: track qualified leads, booked calls, and customers
- Apps: track beta signups, activation, and retention
- Use watch time and CTR as diagnostic signals, not the final definition of success

---

### TESTING
- Define audience, objective, attribution window, primary metric, budget cap, and evaluation rule before launch
- Keep comparisons controlled where practical
- Do not declare a winner from a handful of purchases or clicks
- Report uncertainty and distinguish attributed results from demonstrated incremental lift
