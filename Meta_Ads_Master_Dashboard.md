## 1. Executive Summary

This master dashboard consolidates the Meta Ads campaign strategies for all six businesses under Illustrators Ent LLC. Each business operates with a distinct audience, funnel architecture, and revenue model — from a national AI fitness app ($20K/mo ad spend) to hyper-local Palm Beach County hauling services ($1,800/mo). The total monthly advertising investment across all businesses is **$35,300/month**, allocated based on revenue potential, audience scale, and stage of business.

### Total Monthly Ad Spend Breakdown

| Business | Monthly Budget | % of Total | Primary Objective | Revenue Target |
|----------|---------------|------------|-------------------|----------------|
| Athos Peaks | $20,000 | 56.7% | App registrations & subscriptions | $50K+/mo MRR (target) |
| Juke The Movement | $4,000 | 11.3% | Streaming, merch, and brand growth | $10K+/mo |
| Only The Finest (OTF) | $5,000 | 14.2% | Shopify sales & repeat purchases | $5K-$10K/mo net |
| Herm's Heavy Hauling | $1,800 | 5.1% | Booked cleanout jobs ($3K-$10K each) | $15K-$30K/mo |
| Big Brother Property Solutions | $2,000 | 5.7% | Property service leads & contracts | $10K-$25K/mo |
| Momentum Labs | $2,500 | 7.1% | Brand authority & lead generation | $5K-$15K/mo |
| **TOTAL** | **$35,300** | **100%** | | |

### Key Strategic Principles (All Businesses)
1. **Funnel-first architecture**: Every business runs TOFU (awareness) -> MOFU (consideration) -> BOFU (conversion) campaigns
2. **Creative is the #1 lever**: Real product/brand visuals outperform stock 3:1. Authenticity over polish.
3. **Advantage+ first**: Let Meta's algorithm find audiences before manual interest targeting
4. **Conversions API is mandatory**: Up to 30% of iOS events are lost without CAPI — every business needs it
5. **Frequency management**: Cap weekly frequency to prevent creative fatigue (3.0-4.0 depending on stage)
6. **20% scaling rule**: Never increase budget by more than 20% in a single edit — it resets Meta's learning phase
7. **Data-driven attribution**: UTM parameters on every ad destination, cross-referenced with GA4 or platform analytics
