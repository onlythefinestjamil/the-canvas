## Tiered logo system — REGULAR vs ELITE
- **canvas-icon-wave.png** — REGULAR tier mark (default, free users).
  Wave "C", purple (#9146FF) -> cyan (#38B6FF) gradient.
- **canvas-hero-ribbon.png** — ELITE tier mark (paid subscribers).
  Interwoven silver+teal ribbon knot, frame-corner "A" wordmark.

The UI swaps the mark per the user's subscription tier (free/regular users see
the Wave C; ELITE (paid) subscribers get the Ribbon across the app,
exports, membership card, etc.). "powered by Momentum
Labs" lockup stays constant on both.
