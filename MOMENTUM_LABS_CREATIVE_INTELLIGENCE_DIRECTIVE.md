### CREATIVE REFERENCE LIBRARY (absorbed November 2, 2026)

#### MUSIC CONTENT FORMATS (Rapville framework, tested across 250+ campaigns)
- S-Tier: BTS vs Shot (5/5 virality), Reaction Videos (5/5 conversion)

- D-Tier (avoid): Text Hooks, Mini Music Videos

#### THE "ONE PERSON" FRAMEWORK
- Before filming: write one paragraph about the one person this content is for
- Age, environment, what they scroll, what they share, what they send at midnight, why they listen
- Visual world + hook + format all flow from knowing your one person
- Sharing is the engine for virality, not the algorithm

#### HDPC FRAMEWORK (highest-converting Reels structure)
1. Hook (0-3s) — pattern interrupt, bold statement, trending audio
2. Demonstration (3-15s) — product in action, transformation
3. Proof (15-25s) — social proof, testimonials, before/after
4. CTA (25-30s) — one clear next step

#### TOP HOOKS (2026 OpusClip data, 1.9M clips)
- Specific Outcome (45% hook rate): "I went from X to Y in N days"
- POV (42% hook rate): "POV: you just found the hack"
- Contrarian (38% hook rate): "Unpopular opinion: [belief] is wrong"
